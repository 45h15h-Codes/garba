## Hold for exact-release review

- **`bansari-raas-1996`** — The official label’s Part 1 (`iokqcSy5yvs`, 30:36) + Part 2 (`FipF3h7pawk`, 30:54) pair fits the release family and roughly fits the album runtime, but the catalogue says 19 tracks and the two uploads expose 18 chapters. Do not import as complete until the missing/unchaptered track is resolved. The newer one-video label reissue (`-cIcpIPQPI8`, 1:01:32, 18 chapters) has the same discrepancy.
- **`khelaiya-disco-dandia-93-1993`** — YouTube shows a verified Ishtar Gujarati “Khelaiya Vol. 1” jukebox around 58:55 and official Topic track routes, plus community playlists for Vol. 93. The search evidence does not establish that the official jukebox is the catalogue’s exact Vol. 93 master, so no route is recommended yet.
- **`de-taali-1999`** — A roughly 61-minute community upload titled “De Taali - Rang Taali” appeared, but it does not identify the catalogued “26 Non Stop Dandia Hits” release or a verified label source. Hold.
- **`dhholi-vol15-2011`** — Search surfaced a similarly named *Dholi (Tahuko 18)* rather than the catalogue’s *Dhholi (Non Stop Garba, Vol. 15)*. The volume mismatch prevents an exact mapping.

## Research caveats

- “Integrated” here means a YouTube route exists in the website’s canonical release source list or its linked full-set discovery record. This pass found no such route for any of the confirmed candidate IDs above.
- Search-result runtimes are rounded where noted. Exact player runtimes are reported when visible on the watch page.
- This is a linkage research report, not a claim that the website database has been changed. Keep each continuous upload as one master release; its timestamp chapters are internal navigation metadata, not additional songs/releases.
