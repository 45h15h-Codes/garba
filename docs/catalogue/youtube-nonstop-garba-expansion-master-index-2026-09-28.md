# YouTube Nonstop Garba Expansion — Master Index

**Updated:** 2026-09-28  
**Research surface:** YouTube rendered search, playlist, and video pages in the browser. The user asked for YouTube-only research; Google, Spotify, Apple Music, and other services were not used for this sweep. Videos were not played.  
**Unit:** One continuous full-length upload is one programme/master. Chapter rows inside it are not separate songs. Parts and alternate uploads of one release need grouping before counting parent works.

## Current coverage snapshot

- The current website audit covers **83 nonstop catalogue releases** plus five separately recognized live-event releases. It reports 152 exact YouTube video IDs across the existing site data (some are short/chapter routes or repeated evidence, not 152 masters).
- The ten additive YouTube expansion reports linked below document **454 distinct exact video IDs** after deduplicating identical IDs across reports. This includes cross-referenced routes, multipart uploads, alternates, event recordings, and some held candidates; it is a route count, not a claim of 454 unique nonstop albums.
- Comparing those IDs against the website audit yields **47 exact IDs already present** and **407 IDs absent from the audit’s exact YouTube-route list**. An absent ID may be a new programme, an alternate route for a catalogue work, one part of a multipart programme, or a held candidate; it is not automatically a new parent album.
- Parent programme totals remain lower and provisional until parts and alternate editions are grouped using title, artist, year, runtime, chapter set, and upload evidence. The reports distinguish full nonstop masters, multipart set recordings, live-event nights, shorter medleys, and uncertain search leads.
- The focused reports' internal counts overlap and are **not additive**: 104 artist-channel routes, 120 Tahuko/Tahukar routes, 59 Ramzat/Rangtaali/Khelaiya/Kirtidan routes, 78 IDs from live-event/Ishtar research, 27 Studio Sangeeta routes, and 13 other-label additions. Later artist-series and regional-label addenda broaden the documented exact-route union to 454.

This exceeds the requested 300–400 discovery-link target while keeping programmes distinct from chapters. It does not establish an exhaustive count of all YouTube Garba or of unique parent releases. Release-family totals remain provisional until each multipart upload and alternate edit is grouped against catalogue identities.

### New 50K minimum view-count gate

The user requests a hard minimum of **50,000 displayed YouTube views** for popularity-qualified songs/programmes. The Studio Sangeeta audit (`youtube-popularity-studio-sangeeta-2026-09-28.md`) found 20 of 24 long-form Garba/folk programme rows above the gate, with four below, plus two separate standalone songs that pass. Counts are dated snapshots. A view count not captured for an exact route is **unverified**, not an automatic pass. The other reports continue to add audited/below/held counts without treating chapters as songs.

## Use-before-integration checklist

1. Match exact video IDs against the current YouTube linkage tables.
2. Group parts and alternate uploads under one parent album/event release where titles and metadata support it.
3. Keep full nonstop programmes as one set-level master. Store chapter labels/timestamps only inside that master where the catalogue needs scrubbing/lyrics.
4. Mark match state per parent release: **already linked**, **new alternate route**, **unlinked existing release**, **new catalogue candidate**, or **held for edition/runtime mismatch**.
5. Keep ordinary 2–3 minute singles outside this full-set expansion. Keep live event nights labelled as live sets, separate from studio albums.
