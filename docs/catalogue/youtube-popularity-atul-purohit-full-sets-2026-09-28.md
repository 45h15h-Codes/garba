## Exclusions and cautions

- The official “Most Popular Garba” playlist reports **81 videos** and **3 unavailable/hidden videos** at the playlist level. Its aggregate playlist view count is not a substitute for per-video views.
- Many channel playlist songs have 3–13 minute runtimes and are full song performances. They are not included in the continuous-album table above. Two-to-three-minute teasers/promos are excluded.
- A broad Soor Mandir search also showed *2 Tali Non Stop Garba 2021* (1:32, 1.4M, 32 chapters), but its visible title did not establish Atul Purohit as a credited artist. It is a strong multi-artist/label Garba lead, not an Atul-specific match.
