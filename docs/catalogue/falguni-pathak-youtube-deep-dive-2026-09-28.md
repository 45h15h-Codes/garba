# Falguni Pathak on YouTube: Garba and Nonstop Deep Dive

**Research checked:** 2026-09-28  
**Platform scope:** YouTube only. I inspected Falguni Pathak’s verified artist channel, Topic channel upload/album pages, label-channel uploads, official artist playlists, and relevant YouTube search results.  
**How to count:** A continuous nonstop recording or live event-night recording is **one YouTube programme**. Chapter names inside it are not counted as separate YouTube videos. For the usable standalone-song inventory, I included complete videos **4 minutes or longer**; short 1–3 minute Topic uploads and clips are excluded so they cannot be mistaken for full song masters.  
**Coverage limit:** YouTube search and channel pages do not offer a complete export of every upload credited to an artist. This is a broad, source-checked inventory of Garba-related results I could identify, not a guarantee that every private, unlisted, region-blocked, or poorly indexed upload has been found.

**Full-length rule for integration:** Individual songs must be at least 4:00 and clearly Garba/raas or Garba-devotional. Continuous albums, nonstop mixes, and long event recordings remain one programme each. I’ve intentionally removed short song cuts from the playable inventory.

- The verified [Falguni Pathak artist channel](https://www.youtube.com/@FalguniPathakNavratri) showed **499K subscribers and 181 videos**. Its Playlists tab exposed nine lists. - Expanded YouTube searches surfaced multiple full-length nonstop/set masters, including official-channel/Topic/label uploads, older 2018 sets, and third-party compilations. Duplicate or alternate masters are kept separate where the video IDs differ; their audio has not been compared.
- The [Falguni Pathak, Sohini Mishra - Topic](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCI5jgWFd-XfQbvv5e8GEcng/videos) page showed **17 videos** (16 song titles plus an intro). Those individual cuts are only 1:33–3:38, so I reviewed but excluded them from the usable full-length list under your cutoff.

I included only Garba-related tracks that surfaced with a Garba title/description or in an identified Garba release; I excluded unrelated pop hits.
