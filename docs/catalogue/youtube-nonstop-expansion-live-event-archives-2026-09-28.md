# YouTube Nonstop Garba Expansion — Live Events and Label Playlists

Research date: 2026-09-28  
Scope: YouTube pages opened in the browser. Count distinct full-length YouTube video IDs, not chapters or playlist appearances. This addendum expands the prior site inventory and remains subject to ID-level deduplication against other research files.

## Inclusion rules

- Core candidates are full-length nonstop albums or complete multi-hour Garba event-night recordings. A single full video remains one programme even when YouTube shows chapters.
- Exclude regular short 2–3 minute song clips, shorts, promos, repeated versions of the same YouTube ID, and segmented fragments that do not provide a complete programme.
- Some album jukeboxes are 55–59 minutes; they are retained when the title and official label playlist establish that they are continuous/nonstop sets. Live recordings marked as a part or fragment are separately excluded from the full-programme count.
- “Full event-night recording” is a continuous live event set, not necessarily a studio “nonstop” album. It is clearly labelled as a live set below.
