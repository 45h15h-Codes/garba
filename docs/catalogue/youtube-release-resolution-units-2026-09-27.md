# YouTube Backlog by Release Resolution Unit

Research snapshot: 2026-09-27
Scope: YouTube routes only. This is a release-level tracker; it does not change the catalogue.

## Correct work count

The catalogue reports **295 unlinked rows across 58 affected releases**. Those rows resolve into **58 release-level work units**:

- **28 continuous albums/live programmes**, with 265 internal chapter or segment rows.
- **30 one-row releases**, comprising standalone singles and already-collapsed continuous-set records.

This distinction fixes the misleading “295 songs” phrasing. A continuous master counts once as a work. Its internal chapters only become usable chapter routes when the YouTube publisher exposes verified timestamps; an official album playlist may instead link its separately published tracks. Neither case makes the chapter rows independent catalogue works.

The chapter totals below reproduce the release-level breakdown. The live catalogue is authoritative for implementation.

## Counting decision / next audit

The 28 continuous entries sum to 265 chapter rows. The standalone singles and individual continuous set records contain 30 records. Thus `265 + 30 = 295` and all 58 affected release IDs are represented.

Routes in this tracker are research findings or candidates. Resolve each release with one accepted master or album playlist strategy and preserve exact chapter boundaries only when their timestamps are published and verified.
