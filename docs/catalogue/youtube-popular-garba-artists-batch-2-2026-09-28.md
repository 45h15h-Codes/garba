# YouTube Popular Garba Artists — Batch 2

**Research date:** 2026-09-28  
**Surface:** YouTube search, channel, playlist, and video pages in the browser.  
**Popularity gate:** at least **50,000 displayed views** for inclusion in the qualifying tables. View counts are rounded snapshots and can change.  
**Scope:** Aditya Gadhvi, Jigardan Gadhavi (Jigrra), Kirtidan Gadhvi, Aishwarya Majmudar, and Geeta Rabari as an adjacent popular artist. Includes continuous studio Garba/Raas programmes and long event recordings. Short standalone songs and short clips are excluded from the full-set counts.

## How to read this report

- A continuous nonstop album or programme is **one work**, even when YouTube shows chapters or song transitions inside it.
- Event videos labelled “Day N,” “Part N,” or “Live” are event recordings, not studio album releases. Treat separate parts as one event family when evidence supports that grouping.
- The 50K floor is a discovery filter, not a quality guarantee. Artist/label source and complete-programme evidence are recorded separately.
- Exact YouTube IDs are included only when exposed and verified in the browser or an existing research file. A hidden ID is recorded as unresolved; no URL is guessed.
- A result with 50K+ views can still be held if it is only a partial event, its Garba identity is unclear, or its channel/version relationship is uncertain.
