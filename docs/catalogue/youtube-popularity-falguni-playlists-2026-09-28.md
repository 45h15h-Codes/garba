- No short song clips are counted as nonstop programmes. The 50K threshold alone is not a release-identity, quality, or rights check.
- The captured 2024 playlist is not a complete guarantee of every performance uploaded by Falguni Pathak. This report covers only the two opened playlist pages; wider artist-channel searches continue in the companion [Falguni Pathak/Tahuko audit](youtube-popularity-artist-tahuko-2026-09-28.md).
