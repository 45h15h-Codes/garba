# Ask Kukdu

Kukdu is PlayGarba's rooster guide, designed for the player in Gujarati Navratri colours. Open him from the persistent lower-right rooster button, directly above the YouTube attribution, in Simple or Immersive. The launcher and sheet use the same brass fill as Play, with a deep-maroon header and controls and warm-ivory answer cards. That gives the guide a clear visual connection to the player and keeps the answer text easy to read.

The home screen offers a few starting topics, a song and artist search prompt, and a **Browse all 37 answers** guide grouped by music and player controls, Immersive, Garba and steps, and using Kukdu. Every curated answer has a visible question in the guide; “What's playing?” is a live answer from the current player state. Free-form questions still work. If Kukdu cannot ground an answer in the catalogue or PlayGarba's own help and culture pages, it says so and offers a way to request it. It does not promise to know every possible question.

It borrows the structure of BookPhysio's support surface (`ruddvz/bookphysio`, `docs/planning/support-bot/`), not its look:

- a launcher that opens a panel;
- a home state that is product UI, not a fake message;
- topic chips, a searchable composer and a categorized guide to every curated answer;
- a composer at the bottom;
- a fixed decision order: deterministic routing first, grounded answers, honest escalation;
- a gold set of real questions that the answers are tested against.

Colours, type and spacing are PlayGarba's own: the player accent (`--accent`), maroon and ivory from the player, and the shared Kukdu avatar.

## Files

| Part | File |
| --- | --- |
| Engine and panel | `assets/runtime/ask-playgarba.js` (loaded deferred from `index.html`; not part of `app.js`) |
| Curated answers, synonyms, topic chips and browsable questions | `assets/runtime/ask-intents.json` (hand-written) |
| Page index | `assets/runtime/ask-pages.json` (generated by `npm run ask:pages` from 13 help and culture pages) |
| Gold set | `scripts/lib/test-ask-playgarba.mjs` (`npm run ask:test`) |
| Contract | `scripts/lib/validate-ask-playgarba.mjs`, plus a freshness check of the page index (`npm run ask:validate`) |

## Decision order

Every question goes through the same steps. There is no model and no server.

1. **Normalise.** Lower case, keep Gujarati script, drop possessives, then apply the synonyms. The synonyms cover typos and variants people actually type (`3 taali`, `3rali`, `dodhiya`, `gujrati`, `menimzing`, `arti`), plurals, and a letter or two of misspelling in artist names (`Aditya ghadvi`).
2. **What's playing.** The player's current song, its artist and style. From there it can play more by that artist or save the song.
3. **A curated answer.** One to three sentences that name the control to use, with a button that presses it and a link to the page the answer comes from.
4. **The catalogue.** Artists (their Artist essentials list) and songs, with Play buttons. Songs that can't play show Not available. A named artist or a multi-word title outranks a general feature word. A style named on its own ("Dakla") stays the style, not a one-word song that shares its name.
5. **Our pages.** For questions about the tradition ("why…", "what's the difference…"), the closest section of the help and culture pages is quoted with a link. A page needs at least two matching words to count.
6. **Honest fallback.** A song or singer that isn't here gets "not in PlayGarba yet". Anything else gets "I couldn't find that". Both offer the request form with the question filled in.

## Answer rules

- A live answer names a control that exists today. The validator checks that every button the answers press is really in the player, and that every page they cite exists.
- Anything not built (dance-step animation, an aarti moment, the Gujarati interface, Garba near me, a lights control, artist photos) says **Not yet** and offers the form. It never implies otherwise.
- The guide is generated from the same intent records as free-form routing. The contract check ensures every curated answer appears once, plus the live Now Playing question.
- It never invents a song, a release, an artist fact or a date. Catalogue answers come from the live catalogue; culture answers quote our own pages.
- Plain voice, per `.raas/LANGUAGE.md`: short sentences, no marketing words. The gold set fails on them.

## Privacy

- Questions stay in this tab's `sessionStorage` for an hour, and are re-answered from what's current when the panel reopens.
- Nothing is uploaded, logged or sent. The only requests are the two knowledge files.
- The request form opens only when someone presses its button.

## Placement

- **Simple:** A persistent 48 px rooster button sits directly above YouTube at the lower right, outside More.
- **Laptop:** a side panel under the top bar. **Phone:** a bottom sheet you can drag down, not auto-focused.
- **Keyboard:** Escape closes it, Tab stays inside it, and focus returns to what opened it.
- **Immersive:** A matching persistent 48 px rooster button sits directly above YouTube at the lower right, outside More. It opens the same host panel with `window.parent.postMessage({ type: 'playgarba:ask' }, location.origin)`. The panel also remains available with `window.GARBA_ASK.open()` on the host page.

The header and every answer state show Kukdu's custom rooster avatar. Its profile, comb and mirror-work collar use the player's maroon, brass and ivory palette; it is a drawn mascot, not a chat symbol or emoji.

## Keeping it right

- **A feature ships or changes:** update its intent in `ask-intents.json`, and move it from `not-yet` to `live` with a real action.
- **A help or culture page changes:** run `npm run ask:pages`. `npm run check` fails while the index is stale.
- **The form surfaces a new repeated request:** add the real wording to the gold set first, then the phrase or synonym that answers it.
